The Extension Program Journey

Proposed Programs 2027–2031

A strong Extension Program begins with an identified and validated community need. Before developing a proposal, proponents should examine baseline data and research-based needs assessment results, determine how faculty expertise and academic programs can respond to the identified needs, and design related projects and activities with clear, measurable outcomes. The proposed program should establish a meaningful partnership with the target community or agency and demonstrate potential for sustainability and measurable community impact.

Start with the need, not the activity.
Identify and validate the community problem first, then design the program, projects, and activities as appropriate responses to that need.

Community Satisfaction Survey (CSS) Process

The CSS is administered, processed and reported by the designated Extension Services Office (ESO), not by the project team.

Confidentiality & Data Privacy
  • Beneficiary responses must remain confidential
  • Evaluators and personnel handling beneficiary data must sign an NDA before the survey
  • Survey data must be anonymized so individual respondents cannot be identified

Who administers the CSS?

  • ESO CentralProjects under Central Administration
  • Campus ESOProjects implemented by constituent and extension campuses

IPCR Pointing System for Extension

The Final IPCR Rating is the average of Quality (Q), Efficiency (E) and Timeliness (T).

Quality (Q)

Role in the approved program/project proposal
  • Q5Program/Project Leader
  • Q4Assistant Program/Project Leader
  • Q3Project Staff
  • Q2Participant
  • Q1No participation in an approved extension program/project

Only faculty members officially reflected in the approved Extension Program/Project Proposal with designated roles receive the corresponding Quality rating.

Efficiency (E)

Based on beneficiary evaluation/satisfaction rating
  • E5Project obtained a 100% beneficiary evaluation/satisfaction rating
  • E4Project obtained a 98%–99% rating
  • E3Project obtained a 97% rating
  • E2Project obtained a 90%–96% rating
  • E1No participation in any approved extension program/project, or a rating below 90%

For projects with multiple activities, the average beneficiary evaluation/satisfaction rating of all completed activities determines the E score.

Example
Activity 1 = 96%, Activity 2 = 98%
Average = (96% + 98%) ÷ 2 = 97%, so E = 3

Timeliness (T)

Submission of required reports/documents
  • T5Within 7 calendar days after the deadline
  • T48–14 calendar days after
  • T315–30 calendar days after
  • T2More than 30 calendar days after
  • T1No participation in an approved extension program/project
